# Case 02 — UrbanThreads Retail

## Business Context

UrbanThreads is a fashion retailer selling three product categories: T-shirts, Premium Shirts, and Jackets.

Total revenue increased in 2026, but management is concerned that gross margin may be weakening.

Management ran a promotion focused heavily on T-shirts and is considering repeating the strategy next year.

## Financial Data

| Product | 2025 Units | 2026 Units | 2025 Price | 2026 Price | 2025 Unit Cost | 2026 Unit Cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| T-shirts | 100,000 | 120,000 | $20 | $21 | $10 | $11 |
| Premium Shirts | 50,000 | 55,000 | $50 | $52 | $27 | $30 |
| Jackets | 20,000 | 15,000 | $120 | $125 | $65 | $72 |

## Additional Information

- Management promoted T-shirts heavily during 2026.
- Jackets generate the highest gross profit per unit.
- Supplier costs increased during the year.
- Management believes higher T-shirt sales volume proves the promotion was successful.

## Management Question

> Should UrbanThreads continue pushing high-volume T-shirt promotions next year?

## Your Task

1. Calculate total revenue for 2025 and 2026.
2. Identify whether revenue growth came mainly from volume, price, or product mix.
3. Calculate gross profit per unit for each product in both years.
4. Identify which product changes are most concerning from a profitability perspective.
5. Explain whether higher T-shirt volume automatically means the promotion was successful.
6. Recommend 3 additional pieces of information management should investigate.
7. Write a short 3–5 sentence FP&A conclusion.

## Thinking Framework

Revenue Growth → Volume / Price / Mix → Unit Economics → Margin Impact → Business Implication → Recommendation

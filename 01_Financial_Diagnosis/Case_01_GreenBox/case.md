# Case 01 — GreenBox Meal Kits

## Business Context

GreenBox is a meal-kit subscription company. Revenue grew strongly in 2026, but operating profit declined.

## Financial Data

| Metric | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Active Customers | 40,000 | 46,000 |
| Avg. Orders / Customer | 8.0 | 8.5 |
| Avg. Revenue / Order | $50.00 | $52.00 |
| Revenue | $16.0m | $20.3m |
| Food & Packaging Cost | $8.0m | $11.2m |
| Delivery Cost | $2.4m | $3.5m |
| Marketing | $1.6m | $2.6m |
| Other Operating Costs | $2.0m | $2.1m |
| Operating Profit | $2.0m | $0.9m |

## Management Question

Management says:

> “Revenue grew by more than 25%, so the business is clearly performing better.”

As the FP&A analyst, assess whether this conclusion is fully supported by the financial results.

## Your Task

1. Identify the 3 most important financial observations.
2. Explain the main revenue drivers.
3. Explain why operating profit declined despite revenue growth.
4. Identify 2–3 possible business reasons behind the margin pressure.
5. Recommend 3 areas management should investigate next.
6. Write a short 3–5 sentence FP&A conclusion.

## Thinking Framework

Observation → Driver → Financial Impact → Business Implication → Recommendation
